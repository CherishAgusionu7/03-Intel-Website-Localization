Sustainability story

From ambition to action, each milestone is a chapter in Intel’s climate journey

2030 · Net Zero Carbon

Net-zero emissions powered by smarter operations

Intel commits to net-zero Scope 1 and 2 emissions by optimizing energy, manufacturing, and renewable sourcing across every facility.

  • Smart factory systems with predictive efficiency
  • Renewable electricity expanded across global operations
  • Clean energy atmosphere with lower carbon intensity

2030 · Water Impact

Restoring water with digital precision

Intel restores more freshwater than it consumes in water-stressed regions through advanced recycling, conservation, and water-smart manufacturing.

  • Closed-loop water systems for fabs
  • Smart metrics for regional restoration
  • Digital circuits guiding water flow

2030 · Zero Waste

Closing loops with a circular economy mindset

Intel reduces landfill waste by designing systems, materials, and logistics that keep products and materials in circulation.

  • Recycling loops enabled by smart tracking
  • Waste-to-resource innovation across fabs
  • Materials recovery embedded into operations

2030 · Renewable Electricity

Fueling innovation with 100% renewable power

Intel accelerates renewable energy use through large-scale sourcing, grid partnerships, and clean energy investments across its global sites.

  • Solar and wind networks powering operations
  • Energy grid intelligence to optimize delivery
  • Cleaner power with a glowing, low-carbon future

2040 · Supply Chain Decarbonization

Building a low-carbon network of sustainable partners

Intel drives emissions reduction across its supply chain through digital collaboration, low-carbon materials, and shared climate action.

  • Connected supplier network with carbon signals
  • Global logistics designed for sustainability
  • Decarbonization as a collaborative platform
